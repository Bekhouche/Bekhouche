## About me

I am a Member of Technical Staff at QuTwo in Helsinki, working on AI and quantum computing systems. Before that, I spent three years as Senior Engineer at Huawei's European Research Institute, where I worked on diffusion-based generation of images, video, and LiDAR data on distributed GPU and NPU infrastructure.

I hold two PhDs: Computer Engineering (University of the Basque Country, 2025) and Signals and Communications (University of Biskra, 2017). My research covers generative models, medical image analysis, facial analysis, and Arabic NLP, with 69 publications.

At Huawei, I led the OCR project (2023), contributed to the video generation foundation model for Huawei Cloud (2024), and led the autonomous driving project for video and LiDAR data generation (2025 to 2026). The work included:

1. Diffusion pipelines for image, video, and LiDAR generation, for both training and inference.
2. Multi-GPU and multi-node training of large generative models with DDP, FSDP, and custom strategies.
3. Optimization of attention and compute-heavy operators on GPU and Ascend NPU, with mixed precision and memory optimization.

## Research areas

- **Generative models**: diffusion and rectified flow for video, LiDAR, and multi-sensor data generation.
- **Medical image analysis**: segmentation with diffusion transformers, and lung severity scoring.
- **Remote sensing**: hyperspectral image classification with vision transformers and Mamba.
- **Facial analysis and biometrics**: age estimation, kinship verification, drowsiness detection, affect recognition, and presentation attack detection.
- **Vision-language and Arabic NLP**: pedestrian attribute recognition with VLMs, Arabic retrieval, LLM reasoning, and hallucination detection.
